Overview

Protect your smilebefore problems begin

A healthy smile starts with prevention. Regular dental examinations and professional care allow our specialists to identify early signs of decay, gum disease and other oral health concerns before they require complex treatment.

At Dentify, every preventive visit is designed around your individual needs. We use modern equipment, digital imaging and evidence-based techniques to provide accurate diagnosis and effective solutions. From routine check-ups and professional cleaning to advanced gum care, our preventive services help protect your oral health and preserve your natural smile for years to come.

Treatmentsincluded

Our preventive treatments combine advanced diagnostic technology with expert dental guidance to help you keep strong teeth, healthy gums and a confident smile.

01

Digital X-Ray (Radiograph)

Clear digital imaging to assess teeth, roots, bone levels and areas not visible during examination.

02

Dental Examination + X-Ray

A complete oral health assessment to identify concerns and plan the right next steps.

03

Deep Scaling / Root Planing

Thorough cleaning below the gumline to help manage gum disease and reduce inflammation.

04

Scaling & Polishing

Professional cleaning to remove plaque, tartar and surface stains for healthier teeth and gums.

05

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening designed to brighten your smile under clinical supervision.

06

Pediatric Dental Care

Gentle, age-appropriate dental care focused on keeping children comfortable and their smiles healthy.

Regular preventive care keeps your teeth, gums and smile healthy while reducing the risk of future dental problems. Our treatments focus on early detection, professional cleaning and maintaining long-term oral health.

Your visit

What happens duringa dental exam?

During your dental examination, our dentist checks your teeth, gums, bite and overall oral health. Regular check-ups allow potential problems to be detected early and treated before they become more serious.

  1. 01

    Your concerns & history

    We start by listening: any pain or sensitivity, your medical history and what you'd like from your visit.

  2. 02

    Full oral examination

    Your dentist checks every tooth, your gums, bite and soft tissues for early signs of decay or gum disease.

  3. 03

    Digital X-rays

    Where needed, digital X-rays reveal hidden problems such as decay between teeth, bone loss or root issues.

  4. 04

    Cleaning & your plan

    Professional scaling and polishing removes plaque and tartar that brushing can't. We then discuss your hygiene habits and recommend the right preventive treatments.

✚

Preventive care also includes professional scaling and polishing to remove plaque and tartar that regular brushing cannot eliminate. These treatments help protect your gums, prevent cavities and keep your smile fresh, clean and healthy.

Why preventionmatters

  • Find decay and gum disease early, when treatment is quick and simple
  • Avoid more complex and costly treatment later
  • Fresher breath and cleaner, smoother-feeling teeth
  • Removes tea, coffee and surface stains
  • A personalised plan for the whole family
