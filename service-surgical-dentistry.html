Overview

Safe & precisedental surgical care

When a tooth cannot be restored or is causing ongoing problems, extraction may be the best option for protecting your oral health. Our surgical dentistry services cover routine extractions as well as more complex procedures.

We provide simple, difficult, paediatric, wisdom tooth and surgical extractions. Each procedure is assessed individually to determine the safest and most appropriate approach. Our team focuses on patient comfort, careful treatment and clear aftercare guidance throughout the procedure and recovery process.

Protect the clot

Your treatment

What happens duringa dental extraction?

Knowing what to expect helps you feel calm and prepared.

  1. 01

    Examination & X-ray

    Before extraction, your dentist examines the tooth and surrounding area and may take a digital X-ray to understand the tooth's position, roots and surrounding structures.

  2. 02

    Numbing

    Local anaesthesia is used to numb the treatment area, so you feel pressure but not pain.

  3. 03

    Careful removal

    The tooth is carefully removed using the appropriate technique based on its condition and position.

  4. 04

    Aftercare & follow-up

    After treatment, you receive detailed aftercare instructions to support healing. Our team also explains what to expect during recovery and when follow-up care may be required.
