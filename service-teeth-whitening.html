TeethWhitening

Brighter teeth in one visit with safe, dentist-supervised whitening. Ideal before a wedding, an event or just for you.

60–90 minFrom Rs. [FILL]1 visit

What is it?

Professional whitening uses a carefully controlled gel to lift stains caused by tea, coffee, smoking, paan and age. We protect your gums first, so the result is brighter without damaging your teeth.

Most people see a clear difference after one session. We also offer custom take-home trays to top up your shade. Whitening works on natural teeth only, not on crowns, veneers or fillings, so we check what's right for you first.

Benefits

  • Noticeably whiter teeth, often in one visit
  • Safer and more even than shop-bought kits
  • Gums protected throughout
  • Advice to keep your new shade longer

What to expect

  1. 01

    Shade check

    We check your teeth and gums and record your starting shade.

  2. 02

    Clean & protect

    Teeth are polished and a protective barrier is placed over your gums.

  3. 03

    Whitening

    Whitening gel is applied in short rounds until we reach a natural-looking shade.

  4. 04

    Aftercare

    We share foods to avoid for 48 hours and tips to keep teeth bright.
