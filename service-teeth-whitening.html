Overview

A brighter smile,safely done

Tea, coffee, smoking and time can all leave teeth looking dull or yellow. Professional whitening lifts these stains far more effectively and evenly than shop-bought kits, while keeping your teeth and gums protected.

At Dentify, every whitening treatment starts with a check-up to make sure your teeth and gums are healthy. We record your starting shade, protect your gums and use professional-grade gel under clinical supervision, so your result looks bright and natural rather than artificial. Whitening works on natural teeth only, so we'll also plan around any crowns, veneers or fillings.

Your options

In-clinic or take-home?

Both are supervised by your dentist. Many patients combine them: a clinic session for fast results, then a take-home kit to keep them.

Fastest results

In-clinic whitening

A professional-strength gel is applied in the chair while your gums are protected, with visible results in a single visit of about 60–90 minutes.

Speed of results
Convenience

Often chosen for: weddings, events and anyone who wants a quick, noticeable change

Gentle & flexible

Take-home whitening

Custom-fitted trays made from impressions of your teeth, used with a lower-strength gel at home for a short time each day over one to two weeks.

Convenience

Your treatment

What happens duringteeth whitening?

A simple, comfortable treatment from start to finish.

  1. 01

    Check-up & shade

    We check that your teeth and gums are healthy, discuss your goals and record your starting shade.

  2. 02

    Clean & protect

    Teeth are polished to remove surface stains, and a protective barrier is placed over your gums.

  3. 03

    Whitening

    Professional whitening gel is applied in short rounds until we reach a bright, natural-looking shade.

  4. 04

    Aftercare

    We share which foods and drinks to avoid for 48 hours and how to keep your smile bright.

☼

For the first 48 hours, avoid tea, coffee, cola and other dark foods and drinks. This is when teeth pick up stains most easily.
