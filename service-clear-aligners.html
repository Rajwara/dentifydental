ClearAligners

Straighten your teeth discreetly with removable, nearly invisible aligners. No wires, no brackets.

6–18 monthsFrom Rs. [FILL]Visits every 6–8 weeks

What is it?

Clear aligners are a series of thin, custom-made plastic trays that move your teeth a little at a time. You wear each set for one to two weeks before moving to the next, until your teeth are in place.

They suit teens and adults with crowding, gaps or mild to moderate bite problems. Because they're removable, you can eat what you like and brush normally. You just need to wear them 20–22 hours a day.

Benefits

  • Almost invisible, great for work and photos
  • Removable for eating and brushing
  • Fewer emergency visits than braces
  • See a preview of your final result before you start

What to expect

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We examine your teeth, take photos and X-rays, and check whether aligners suit you.

  2. 02

    Digital plan

    Your teeth are scanned and we plan each movement, so you can see the expected result.

  3. 03

    Wear your aligners

    You switch to a new set every one to two weeks and visit us regularly for check-ins.

  4. 04

    Retainers

    Once your teeth are straight, retainers keep them in their new position.
