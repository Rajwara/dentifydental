Overview

Straighter teeth,barely noticed

Clear aligners are a series of thin, custom-made trays that gently move your teeth a little at a time. You wear each set for one to two weeks before moving to the next, until your teeth reach their planned position.

At Dentify, aligner treatment starts with a detailed assessment and a digital scan of your teeth, so we can plan each movement precisely and show you the expected result before you begin. Aligners suit many teens and adults with crowding, gaps or mild bite problems, and because they're removable, you can eat what you like and keep brushing normally.

A day with aligners

Worn 22 hours, out only for meals

Clear aligners work quietly in the background while you work, study and sleep. You simply take them out to eat, then brush and pop them back in.

  • Wear your aligners 20–22 hours a day
  • Remove them to eat or drink anything but water
  • Brush your teeth before putting them back in
  • Switch to a new set every one to two weeks

Your options

Aligners or braces?

Both straighten teeth very effectively. The right choice depends on your teeth, your bite and your lifestyle.

Nearly invisible

Clear aligners

Removable, custom-made clear trays that move your teeth gradually. Eat what you like and brush normally, but wear them 20–22 hours a day.

Discreet look
Suits complex cases

Often chosen for: adults and teens with crowding, gaps or mild bite issues who want a discreet option

Works for more cases

Fixed braces

Brackets and wires fixed to your teeth, in metal or tooth-coloured ceramic. They work around the clock without you having to remember to wear them.

Discreet look
Suits complex cases

Often chosen for: more complex bite problems, and patients who prefer not to manage removable trays

Your treatment

What happens duringaligner treatment?

A simple, step-by-step journey to a straighter smile.

  1. 01

    Assessment & scan

    We examine your teeth, take photos and X-rays, and capture a digital 3D scan to check whether aligners suit you.

  2. 02

    Your digital plan

    We plan each tooth movement and show you the expected result, along with your timeline and cost.

  3. 03

    Wear your aligners

    You switch to a new set every one to two weeks and visit us regularly so we can check your progress.

  4. 04

    Retainers

    Once your teeth are straight, retainers keep them in their new position.

≋

Aligners only work while you wear them. Keeping them in for 20–22 hours a day is the most important thing you can do for a great result.

Why chooseclear aligners

  • Almost invisible, great for work, study and photos
  • Removable for eating, drinking and brushing
  • Smooth plastic, with no wires or brackets
  • Preview your expected result before you start
  • Fewer emergency visits than fixed braces
