Crowns &Bridges

Restore the strength, shape and look of damaged teeth, or replace a missing tooth with a fixed bridge.

2 visitsFrom Rs. [FILL] / unitLong-lasting

What is it?

A crown is a custom cap that covers a weak, cracked, heavily filled or root-canal-treated tooth, protecting it and restoring its shape. A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring false teeth to the teeth on either side.

We offer tooth-coloured options such as zirconia and porcelain, matched to your natural shade, so your smile looks seamless.

Benefits

  • Protects weak or cracked teeth from breaking
  • Restores comfortable chewing
  • Natural, tooth-coloured materials
  • Fixed in place, no removing at night

What to expect

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We check the tooth, take X-rays and choose the best material with you.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    The tooth is shaped, impressions or scans are taken, and a temporary crown is fitted.

  3. 03

    Fitting

    Your permanent crown or bridge is checked for fit, bite and colour, then cemented.

  4. 04

    Care tips

    We show you how to clean around it, especially under a bridge.
