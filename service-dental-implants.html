Overview

A tooth that feelslike your own

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up. A small titanium post is placed in the jawbone, where it bonds with the bone, and a natural-looking crown is fixed on top.

At Dentify, implant treatment begins with a thorough examination and digital X-rays to check your bone and plan the ideal position. Because an implant doesn't rely on the teeth beside it, your healthy teeth stay untouched, and with good care an implant can last for many years. We'll explain every step, the timeline and the cost before treatment begins.

Your treatment

What happens duringimplant treatment?

Implant treatment happens in stages over a few months, with plenty of time to heal.

  1. 01

    Consultation & X-rays

    We examine your mouth and take digital X-rays to check your bone and plan the implant's position.

  2. 02

    Implant placement

    The implant post is placed under local anaesthetic. Most people say it feels easier than they expected.

  3. 03

    Healing

    Over a few months the implant bonds with the bone. A temporary tooth can be fitted in the meantime if needed.

  4. 04

    Your final crown

    Your custom crown, bridge or denture is attached and adjusted for a comfortable, natural bite.

◎

Healthy gums and enough bone are important for implants. If you smoke, stopping before and after treatment greatly improves healing.
