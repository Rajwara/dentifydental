DentalImplants

A permanent, natural-looking way to replace one tooth, several teeth or a full arch.

3–6 monthsFrom Rs. [FILL]Several visits

What is it?

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone, where it acts like a tooth root. After it heals and bonds with the bone, a crown, bridge or denture is fixed on top.

Implants look, feel and work like natural teeth, and they don't rely on the teeth next to them for support. With good care they can last for many years. Healthy gums and enough bone are needed; if bone is thin, grafting may help.

Benefits

  • Feels and works like a natural tooth
  • No need to cut down neighbouring teeth
  • Helps protect the jawbone from shrinking
  • Eat and smile with confidence

What to expect

  1. 01

    Consultation & scan

    We examine your mouth and take X-rays or a 3D scan to check bone and plan placement.

  2. 02

    Implant placement

    The implant is placed under local anaesthetic. Most people say it feels easier than an extraction.

  3. 03

    Healing

    Over a few months the implant bonds with the bone. A temporary tooth can be fitted if needed.

  4. 04

    Final crown

    Your custom crown, bridge or denture is attached and adjusted for a comfortable bite.
