Your privacy matters

At Dentify Dental & Orthodontic Studio, we respect your privacy and recognise the importance of protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect through our website, how we may use and protect that information, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with Dentify and our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Appointment or consultation requests
  • Treatment of interest
  • Messages or enquiries you submit
  • Information you voluntarily provide when communicating with us
  • Basic technical information relating to website usage, such as browser, device and interaction data

Please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive medical or personal information through general website forms.

2. How we use your information

Information collected through our website may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Process and manage appointment requests
  • Contact you regarding your appointment or enquiry
  • Provide information about relevant dental services
  • Support patient communication and administration
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve our website
  • Meet applicable professional, regulatory or legal obligations

We aim to use personal information only for legitimate purposes connected with your interaction with Dentify.

3. Patient & health information

Information obtained as part of your clinical care may include dental history, medical history, diagnostic information, X-rays, photographs, treatment records, prescriptions and other information relevant to your care.

Clinical information is handled with appropriate confidentiality and is accessed or used as reasonably necessary for providing and administering dental care or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

4. Website forms

When you submit an enquiry or appointment form, the information you provide is transmitted to Dentify so our team can respond to your request.

Submitting a form does not itself create or confirm a dentist–patient appointment.

5. Cookies & website analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand how visitors use the website, improve performance and measure website traffic.

Where third-party analytics or similar technologies are used, those providers may process certain technical information according to their own privacy practices.

You may be able to restrict or disable cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may affect certain website functions.

6. How we share information

Dentify does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with service providers that help us operate our website, communications, booking systems, IT infrastructure or other business functions.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, regulatory requirements, legal proceedings, or where necessary to protect legitimate rights or safety.

Where service providers process personal information on our behalf, we expect them to handle it appropriately and only for authorised purposes.

7. Data security

We take reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet applicable clinical, administrative, professional or legal requirements.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information involved.

9. Third-party websites

Our website may contain links or integrations involving third-party services such as maps or social media platforms. These services operate under their own privacy policies, and Dentify is not responsible for their independent privacy practices.

10. Your information

You may contact Dentify if you would like to ask about personal information you have provided to us or request that inaccurate information be corrected.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for clinical, professional, administrative or legal purposes.

11. Children's information

Where dental services are provided to children or minors, information may be collected and managed through a parent, guardian or other authorised person as appropriate.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our website, technology, clinic practices or applicable requirements.

Any revised version will be published on this page with the latest revision date.
