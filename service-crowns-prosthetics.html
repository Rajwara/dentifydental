Overview

Restore your smile,strength & function

Missing, damaged or heavily worn teeth can affect your appearance, confidence, chewing and speech. Our crowns, veneers and removable prosthetic treatments are designed to restore both dental function and aesthetics.

We provide premium Zirconia and E-Max restorations for patients seeking durable and natural-looking solutions. Custom removable dentures are also available for patients with multiple or complete tooth loss. Every restoration is planned according to your dental condition, comfort and functional needs to achieve a secure fit and natural appearance.

Materials

Zirconia or E-Max?

Both are premium, metal-free ceramics. Your dentist will recommend the right one for each tooth.

Strong & durable

Zirconia

Your treatment

What happens duringcrown & prosthetic treatment?

Every restoration is custom-made for you. Here's how it works.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Your dentist first examines the condition of your teeth, gums and bite before recommending the appropriate restoration.

  2. 02

    Preparation & measurements

    For crowns and veneers, the tooth is carefully prepared before measurements are taken for a custom restoration. For removable dentures, impressions and measurements are used to create a personalised fit.

  3. 03

    Fitting

    The final restoration is fitted and adjusted for comfort, bite and function.

  4. 04

    Confidence restored

    Your restoration is designed to help you eat, speak and smile with greater confidence. We'll show you how to care for it.

▣

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, crowns, veneers and dentures can serve you well for many years.

FAQ

Crowns & prostheticsquestions

Can't find what you need? Call us at +92 325 7766667.

A crown covers the whole tooth to protect and strengthen it. A veneer is a thin shell bonded to the front of a tooth to improve its colour, shape or size.

Zirconia is extremely strong and is often chosen for back teeth. E-Max offers the most lifelike translucency and is often chosen for front teeth and veneers. Your dentist will recommend the best option for each tooth.

With good care and regular check-ups, ceramic crowns and veneers can last many years. Avoid biting very hard objects and wear a night guard if you grind your teeth.

Dentures are made from impressions of your mouth for a personalised fit. It's normal to need a short adjustment period, and we can make small adjustments for comfort.

The tooth is numbed during preparation, so most people feel little more than pressure. Mild sensitivity afterwards usually settles within a few days.
