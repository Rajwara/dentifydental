Overview

Fast help whenyou need it most

Dental emergencies include severe toothache, a cracked or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling of the face or gums, and bleeding that won't stop. Getting seen quickly can relieve pain fast and save a tooth that might otherwise be lost.

Call us first on +92 325 7766667 so we can give you first-aid advice and the earliest time we can see you. We keep time in our day for urgent cases, Monday to Saturday from 10 am to 9 pm. If you have facial swelling with fever, or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest hospital emergency department straight away.

    Know when to act

    How urgent is it?

    A quick guide to help you decide where to go. If in doubt, call us and we'll advise you.

    Go to hospital now
    • Swelling spreading to your eye or neck
    • Difficulty breathing or swallowing
    • High fever with facial swelling
    • Heavy bleeding that won't stop after 20 minutes of pressure
    • A serious injury to the face or jaw
    Call us today
    • A knocked-out adult tooth
    • Severe toothache that won't settle
    • Swelling of the gum or face, without fever
    • A badly broken tooth
    • A loose tooth after an injury
    Book soon
    • A lost filling or crown
    • A small chip with no pain
    • Mild sensitivity to hot or cold
    • Braces wire or bracket issues
    • Mild gum soreness

    Your treatment

    What happens atan emergency visit?

    Our first goal is to get you comfortable. Then we plan the permanent fix.

    1. 01

      Call us

      Tell us what happened. We'll give you first-aid advice and the earliest time we can see you.

    2. 02

      Assessment

      We examine the area and take a digital X-ray to find the cause.

    3. 03

      Relief

      We treat the pain first, with a temporary filling, drainage, splint or other urgent care.

    4. 04

      Follow-up

      We explain the permanent fix and its cost, and book it at a time that suits you.
