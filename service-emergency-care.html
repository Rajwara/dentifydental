EmergencyCare

Toothache, a broken tooth or swelling? Call us and we'll see you as soon as we possibly can.

UrgentFrom Rs. [FILL]Call first

What is it?

Dental emergencies include severe toothache, a cracked or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling of the face or gums, and bleeding that won't stop. Getting seen quickly can save the tooth and stop the pain.

Call us first on +92 325 7766667 so we can prepare for your visit and give you advice on the way. If you have facial swelling with fever or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest hospital emergency department straight away.

Benefits

  • Fast pain relief
  • Better chance of saving a damaged tooth
  • Clear advice over the phone before you arrive
  • A follow-up plan to fix the problem for good

What to expect

  1. 01

    Call us

    Tell us what happened. We'll give you first-aid advice and the earliest time we can see you.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    We examine the area and take an X-ray to find the cause.

  3. 03

    Relief

    We treat the pain first, with a temporary filling, drainage, splint or other urgent care.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    We explain the permanent fix and book it when it suits you.
