Overview

Create a straighter,healthier smile

Orthodontic treatment can improve the position of your teeth, the alignment of your bite and the overall appearance of your smile. We provide treatment options designed for different dental needs and preferences.

Our orthodontic care includes traditional braces, ceramic braces, clear aligners, retainers and night guards. Treatment is planned according to your individual dental condition and desired outcome. With regular monitoring and carefully controlled tooth movement, orthodontic treatment can help create a more balanced smile while improving everyday dental function.

Your treatment

What happens duringorthodontic treatment?

From your first assessment to your final retainer, here's how treatment works.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Treatment begins with a detailed assessment of your teeth and bite. Your dentist evaluates alignment and determines which orthodontic solution is most suitable.

  2. 02

    Your treatment plan

    We explain your options (metal braces, ceramic braces or clear aligners), how long treatment may take and what to expect.

  3. 03

    Active treatment

    Braces or clear aligners gradually move the teeth towards their planned positions. Regular appointments allow progress to be monitored and adjustments to be made when required.

  4. 04

    Retainers

    Once active treatment is complete, retainers help maintain the corrected position of your teeth and protect your results over time.

≋

Teeth naturally try to drift back after treatment. Wearing your retainer as advised is the best way to protect your new smile.
