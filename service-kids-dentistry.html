Overview

Happy visits,healthy smiles

Kids dentistry covers check-ups, gentle cleaning, fluoride varnish, fissure sealants, tooth-coloured fillings and simple advice on brushing and diet. We take our time, explain everything in words children understand and never rush a nervous child.

Milk teeth matter: they help children chew, speak clearly and hold space for adult teeth. Bringing your child in early builds healthy habits and a calm, positive relationship with the dentist that lasts a lifetime. Parents are always welcome to stay with their child throughout the visit.

Growing smiles

When do teeth arrive?

Every child is different, but here's roughly what to expect as your child's smile grows, and when we can help.

  1. 6–10 months

    First tooth

    Usually a lower front tooth. Book your baby's first dental visit around now, or by their first birthday.

  2. 1–3 years

    Milk teeth arrive

    All 20 baby teeth come through by about age three. Start brushing twice a day with a smear of fluoride toothpaste.

  3. 6–7 years

    First adult molars

    The first permanent back teeth appear behind the milk teeth. A great time for protective fissure sealants.

  4. 6–12 years

    Wobbly teeth

    Milk teeth fall out and adult teeth replace them. We keep an eye on spacing and bite as they grow.

  5. 12–13 years

    Second molars

    Most adult teeth are in place. If teeth are crooked, this is often a good time to talk about orthodontics.

Fun at home

Two-minute brushing challenge

Dentists recommend brushing for two minutes, twice a day. Press start and brush along with the timer. It tells you when to move to the next part of your mouth.

Your treatment

What happens atyour child's visit?

We keep every visit short, simple and positive.

  1. 01

    Meet & greet

    Your child gets to see the chair, the light and the tools, and try them out, before anything starts.

  2. 02

    Gentle check

    We count and check teeth, gums and bite, often with little ones sitting on your lap.

  3. 03

    Clean & protect

    A quick clean, plus fluoride varnish or sealants if they're needed.

  4. 04

    Tips for home

    We show you and your child how to brush well and share snack tips for healthier teeth.

◐

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste: a rice-grain-sized smear for under-3s, and a pea-sized amount from age 3. Help with brushing until about age 7.
