KidsDentistry

Gentle, patient care for babies, children and teens, in a clinic that makes visiting the dentist feel easy.

30 minFrom Rs. [FILL]Every 6 months

What is it?

Kids dentistry covers check-ups, cleanings, fluoride varnish, fissure sealants, tooth-coloured fillings and advice on brushing and diet. We take our time, explain everything in simple words and never rush a nervous child.

Milk teeth matter: they hold space for adult teeth and help with chewing and speech. Bringing your child in early builds good habits and a calm relationship with the dentist.

Benefits

  • A friendly first experience that builds trust
  • Fluoride and sealants to help prevent cavities
  • Early check of bite and jaw growth
  • Practical brushing and snack advice for parents

What to expect

  1. 01

    Meet & greet

    Your child gets to see the chair, the light and the tools before anything starts.

  2. 02

    Gentle check

    We count and check teeth, gums and bite, often with your child on your lap if they're little.

  3. 03

    Prevention

    A quick clean, plus fluoride varnish or sealants if they're needed.

  4. 04

    Tips for home

    We show you and your child how to brush well and what to watch out for.
