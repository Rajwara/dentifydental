Overview

A natural smile,crafted for you

Veneers are thin, custom-made ceramic shells bonded to the front of your teeth. They can change colour, shape, length and spacing all at once, transforming your smile with very little change to the tooth underneath.

At Dentify, every veneer is planned around your face, lips and skin tone, so the result looks like you, only brighter and more balanced. We use premium E-Max and Zirconia ceramics that resist stains and reflect light like natural enamel, and we explain every option and cost clearly before treatment begins.

Your treatment

What happens duringveneer treatment?

Most veneer treatments take two to three visits.

  1. 01

    Consultation & smile design

    We discuss what you'd like to change, examine your teeth and gums, and plan the shape and shade of your new smile together.

  2. 02

    Gentle preparation

    A very thin layer of enamel, about 0.5 mm, is prepared from the front of each tooth and precise measurements are taken.

  3. 03

    Custom veneers made

    Your veneers are crafted to your plan. Temporary veneers can be placed in the meantime if needed.

  4. 04

    Bonding & polish

    Your veneers are checked for fit and colour, bonded in place and polished, then we check your bite and comfort.

◇

Veneers resist stains, but they can chip under heavy force. Avoid biting very hard objects, and ask about a night guard if you grind your teeth.
