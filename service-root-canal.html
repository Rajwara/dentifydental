Overview

Save yournatural tooth

A damaged or infected tooth does not always need to be removed. Root canal treatment can help preserve your natural tooth by removing infection from inside the tooth and restoring its function.

At Dentify, root canal treatment is carefully planned using clinical examination and diagnostic X-rays. Our approach focuses on removing infection, relieving discomfort and protecting the remaining tooth structure. Treating an infected tooth early can help prevent the infection from spreading and reduce the risk of further damage. Once treatment is completed, the tooth can be restored to support normal chewing and everyday function.

Inside your tooth

What a root canal actually treats

Deep inside every tooth is the pulp, soft tissue with nerves and blood vessels that runs down through the root canals. When deep decay, a crack or an injury lets bacteria reach the pulp, it becomes inflamed or infected, and that's when pain starts.

Root canal treatment removes the affected pulp, cleans and seals the canals, and keeps the outer tooth in place.

Your treatment

What happens duringroot canal treatment?

Root canal treatment is far more comfortable than its reputation. Here's what to expect, step by step.

  1. 01

    Examination & X-rays

    Your dentist first examines the affected tooth and surrounding tissues. Digital X-rays may be taken to evaluate the roots and determine the extent of the problem.

  2. 02

    Numbing

    Local anaesthesia is used to keep the treatment area comfortable throughout.

  3. 03

    Cleaning & sealing

    The infected or damaged tissue is carefully removed before the root canals are cleaned, shaped and sealed.

  4. 04

    Restoring the tooth

    Depending on the condition of the tooth, a core build-up or crown may be recommended afterwards to provide additional strength and protection.

❖

Most patients say root canal treatment feels much like having a filling. It relieves the pain rather than causing it.
